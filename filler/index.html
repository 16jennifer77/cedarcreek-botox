What it is

Hyaluronic acid, a sugar your skin already makes

Juvederm is a line of hyaluronic acid fillers. Hyaluronic acid is a sugar your skin already makes, and the gel form of it is used to restore subtle volume and smooth the look of folds. It is placed, not injected into a muscle, and it is placed in small amounts.

  • One product family here, the Juvederm line, rather than a menu of brands.
  • Placed by licensed nurses, in a studio, with the same hands each visit.
  • Whether filler suits you at all is decided at your complimentary consultation.

Juvederm fillers are prescription treatments. What suits your face, if anything, and what it costs is talked through plainly at your complimentary consultation. Individual results vary.

One at a time

Built over visits, never in one sitting

The way an overfilled face happens is rarely one bad decision. It is volume added faster than anyone stopped to look at it. So the pacing here is the plan, not a preference.

  • One area to begin. Not a package, not a full face, and not two areas because you are already in the chair.
  • A conservative amount. Placed where it does the most, and no more than that.
  • Reviewed before more is added. You come back, it is looked at in daylight, and only then is anything else considered.

Some visits end with a plan. Some end with a date to look again. If the honest answer is to wait, or to do nothing at all, that is what you will hear, and you will not be charged for hearing it.

Questions

The things people ask before they book

Will I look overfilled?

That is the fear most people carry into the room, and it is answered by how the work is planned rather than by a promise. Filler here starts with one area and a conservative amount, and it is looked at again before anything else is added. Nothing is built in a single sitting. That pacing is the whole reason a result can sit on a face without announcing itself. Individual results vary.

Who places the filler?

Licensed nurses. Christyl, Kelsey, and Stephanie are the RNs who place the injections at Cedar Creek Cosmo, and one of them will be the person who plans your treatment and the person who performs it. The studio has been on the lake since 2020 and is an Allergan Botox Platinum Provider, an award that reflects years of injection experience.

What does it cost, and can I spread it out?

Cost depends on the area and the amount, so it is quoted at your complimentary consultation rather than off a menu, and it is explained before anything is scheduled. Alle payment plans are available here if you would prefer to spread a plan over time instead of paying for it in one visit. Ask about Alle at your consultation and the options will be walked through with you.

Does it hurt?

Comfort is discussed before anything is placed, and what is available to make the visit more comfortable is part of that conversation. What it feels like differs from person to person and from area to area, so your nurse will tell you plainly what to expect in the area you are considering rather than promising you a number.

How long does it last, and what happens when it wears off?

Filler is not permanent. How long a result holds varies by product, by area, and by person, which is why it is discussed for your specific plan at your consultation rather than stated as a figure here. As it fades it fades gradually, and the decision about what comes next is made the same way the first one was: one area, looked at first, and only if it is still the right call for you.

I am not sure whether I need filler or Botox.

That is the most common question in the room, and it is decided at your complimentary consultation by a licensed provider who looks first. Relaxers soften the lines expression makes, and filler restores volume, and a good number of faces need one and not the other.
