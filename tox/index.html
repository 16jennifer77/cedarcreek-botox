Questions

The ones people actually ask

Will I look frozen or overdone?

That is the question this studio hears most, and the answer is in the dosing. Placement is planned to your face and added in small amounts, gradually, so your expressions keep working. Softened, not switched off. Starting smaller than the plan suggests is a normal way to begin. Individual results vary.

Who injects?

Licensed nurses. Christyl, Kelsey, and Stephanie are RNs, and one of them places every injection. Cedar Creek Cosmo is an Allergan Botox Platinum Provider, an award that reflects years of proven Botox experience.

What does it cost?

Relaxers are priced by the amount used, so the number depends on the plan you and your provider agree on. The consultation is complimentary, every cost is gone over before anything is decided, and Alle payment plans are available. Nothing is charged for a plan you decide not to do.

Is there any downtime?

Most people go back to their day. Small marks at the injection points usually settle quickly. Your provider goes over aftercare with you before you leave, and individual results vary.

How long does it last?

Wrinkle relaxers wear off gradually. How long yours lasts depends on your face, the area treated, and the amount used, so your provider gives you a realistic range at the consultation rather than a number off a chart.
