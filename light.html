Questions

Asked from the chair.

Will I look frozen or overdone?

A natural looking result is the goal here. Your provider plans injectables conservatively, with you, so you look like a refreshed version of yourself rather than worked on. Outcomes vary from person to person, and your provider sets honest expectations at your consultation.

What does it cost?

Your initial consultation is complimentary, so you can get answers before committing to anything. Because the right plan depends on the treatment, your provider goes over any costs clearly with you, with no surprises. Call (903) 802-5130 with questions.

Who places the injections?

Cedar Creek Cosmo is provider led. Facial injections are placed by licensed nurses, in the studio on the lake, every visit, by a team that plans to see you again.

Does it hurt, and is there downtime?

Most people find facial injections quick and well tolerated, and many return to their day afterward. Comfort and any downtime depend on the treatment and the person, so your provider explains what to expect for your specific plan before you decide anything.

How long do results last?

It varies by treatment and from person to person. Your provider talks through a typical timeline for what you are considering, and what an upkeep plan might look like, during your consultation.

Is it right for me?

Aesthetic treatments are elective and not right for everyone. What is appropriate is decided at a consultation with a licensed provider, and a complimentary consultation is where that decision is made together. We will be honest about what will help, what won't, and when the answer is to wait.
